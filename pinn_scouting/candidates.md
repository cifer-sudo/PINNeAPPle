## C1 — Ageing- and ambient-robust core-temperature estimation of 21700 cells, validated on embedded thermocouples (two labs)

- **Datasets.** WMG core-temperature ageing (Mendeley bf6fksxpp2, CC BY 4.0: 3 instrumented + 3 pristine LG M50, 550 cycles, 1 Hz, core and surface T) + TU Berlin internal temperature (DepositOnce 25110, CC BY 4.0: 3 LG M50LT, 12 drive cycles x 5 ambient T). Optional: LG HG2 / Panasonic for surface-T-only pre-training.
- **Governing physics in the loss.** Two-state lumped thermal model (core/surface): C_c dT_c/dt = Q - (T_c - T_s)/R_c ; C_s dT_s/dt = (T_c - T_s)/R_c - (T_s - T_amb)/R_u. Heat Q = I (U_ocv - V) + I T dU_ocv/dT (irreversible + entropic), U_ocv(SOC) from pseudo-OCV/RPT; R_c, R_u, C_c, C_s learnable (Arrhenius / ageing dependence of internal resistance through heat generation). Quasi-steady Biot relation T_c = T_s + G (T_s - T_amb) as the minimum physics baseline.
- **Network predicts.** Core temperature trajectory T_c(t) (hidden state) and the thermal parameters (R_c, C_c, R_u, C_s) per cell and ageing stage, with uncertainty.
- **Supervision / held out.** Supervised by V, I, T_s (all cells) and T_c on training instrumented cells; physics residual on the non-instrumented cells (unlabelled collocation). Held out: one instrumented cell (leave-one-cell-out), late-ageing blocks, and the TU Berlin ambient temperatures never seen in training.
- **Why a pure data-driven model is weaker.** Core temperature is unmeasurable in a production cell, so a black box can only be trained on a handful of drilled cells and must extrapolate to other ambients, ageing states and cells; the thermal ODE constrains the dynamics, exposes identifiable parameters, and lets unlabelled cells contribute through the residual.
- **Compute / software / effort.** JAX or PyTorch, CPU is enough (1 Hz, few states). Effort: 10-14 weeks to a submission (2 w data pipeline incl. MATLAB-table conversion, 3 w models + baselines, 3 w OOD/ablation, 3 w writing).

## C4 — Physics-constrained ageing law (SEI sqrt-t calendar + throughput/Arrhenius cycle) extrapolating to unseen temperature and DoD

- **Datasets.** Sandia (Preger 2020; NCA/NMC/LFP x T x DoD x rate) and ISU-ILCC (238 NMC pouch, 63 conditions) via Battery Archive; CALCE PLN storage (144 cells, 4 T x 3 SOC) for calendar ageing.
- **Governing physics in the loss.** dQ_loss/dt = k_cal(T,SOC) / (2 sqrt(t)) + k_cyc(T,C-rate,DoD) |I|; k = k0 exp(-Ea/R (1/T - 1/Tref)); Tafel SOC dependence; knee via lithium-plating term. NN learns residual corrections and condition embeddings while monotonicity and Arrhenius structure are enforced.
- **Network predicts.** Capacity trajectory per cell, kinetic parameters (Ea, k0), knee onset.
- **Supervision / held out.** Capacity checks; hold out whole temperatures / DoD levels / chemistries (leave-one-condition-out).
- **Why a pure data-driven model is weaker.** Lifetime tests never cover the deployment grid; extrapolation in temperature is exactly where black boxes break and where Arrhenius structure helps.
- **Compute / software / effort.** CPU/GPU light; 10-14 weeks.

## C5 — Health-aware electro-thermal ECM evolution learned from random-walk EV-like loads (NASA Randomized Usage)

- **Datasets.** NASA Randomized Battery Usage RW1-RW28 (S3 mirror): random-walk 0.5-5 A loads, 3 load distributions, room T and 40 C; reference capacity, 15 W and pulse tests every 50 RW cycles.
- **Governing physics in the loss.** 1RC/2RC ECM: V = U_ocv(z) - R0(T,h) I - v1, dv1/dt = -v1/(R1 C1) + I/C1, dz/dt = -I/Q(h); Arrhenius R(T); lumped thermal m c dT/dt = I^2 R0 + I v1 - hA (T - T_amb); slow health state h (capacity, R0) evolving with Ah throughput and temperature (monotone).
- **Network predicts.** Per-cycle health state (capacity Q, R0, R1, C1) from operating data only, plus SOC and temperature trajectories.
- **Supervision / held out.** V, I, T from random-walk discharges (all cycles); reference capacity and pulse-derived R0/RC every 50 cycles used ONLY for validation (or sparse supervision ablation). Held out: whole cells, load distributions, ambient (room vs 40 C).
- **Why a pure data-driven model is weaker.** Capacity labels exist only every 50 cycles (13 points per cell): a black-box SOH regressor has almost nothing to learn from, whereas the ECM residual on every random-walk second carries information on Q and R0. The physics also transfers across load distributions and temperature.
- **Compute / software / effort.** 1 GPU or CPU; 1.07 GB data; 10-14 weeks.
